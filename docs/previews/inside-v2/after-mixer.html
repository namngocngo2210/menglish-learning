Extend, Expand, etc.

387 từ

This week on Ask a Teacher, we answer a question from Ann in China. Here is part of Ann’s question:

Hello Teachers, Could you please help me understand the difference between "extend," "expand," "stretch," [and] "enlarge" when I describe an activity that makes something larger than its current state?

Thank you, Ann.

We can use the four verbs Ann asked about to express an increase in physical size or amount. We can also use them to express increasing the time an activity takes.

Extend
We often use the verb “extend” to talk about increasing the amount of time for an activity or program. For example:
Officials want to extend the school year by six weeks.

We can use the verb “extend” to express an increase in people, things, or ideas. Consider the following examples:
The new rule extends to all employees in the company.
